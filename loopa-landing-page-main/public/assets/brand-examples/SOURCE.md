# Sources — /brands real product examples

Real, named products used as credible examples on `/brands`. Not stock photography.

## lamino.webp

Swedese "Lamino" armchair, designed by Yngve Ekström (1956).

- Photo: [File:Y_Ekstroem_lamino.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Y_Ekstroem_lamino.jpg), Wikimedia Commons.
  Licensed CC BY-SA (also GFDL) — reused with attribution, no local modification besides resizing/webp
  re-encoding for page weight. Fetched 2026-08-25.
- Specs (bredd 70 cm, djup 78 cm, höjd 101 cm, sitthöjd 41 cm, material, tillverkningsort Vaggeryd,
  formgivare, lanseringsår) verified via grounded research against swedese.se and retailer sources
  earlier in this project.

## kanken.webp / kanken-product-crop.webp

Fjällräven "Kånken" backpack (Classic, 16L, launched 1978), Vinylon F fabric.

- Photo: [File:Fjällräven Kånken backpacks.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fj%C3%A4llr%C3%A4ven_K%C3%A5nken_backpacks.jpg),
  Wikimedia Commons, photographer Lisa Risager, licensed CC BY-SA 2.0 — reused with attribution.
  Fetched 2026-08-25.
- `kanken-product-crop.webp` is a local crop of the same photo (sharp, `.extract()`), isolating just
  the yellow bag — used as the generic product visual in the "Så enkelt blir det för säljaren" story
  section on `/brands`, where a product-only (no people) image is required. Same license/attribution
  as the source photo above; no other modification.

## Interior example

Real product referenced by name/spec only (Iittala "Alvar Aalto Collection" vase, 160mm, designed by
Alvar Aalto, mouth-blown glass, Iittala glassworks, Finland; ca 1 889 kr for the clear finish per
iittala.com/sv-se, fetched 2026-08-25) — deliberately NOT paired with a studio photo of the vase itself.
The Aalto/Savoy vase's underlying design remains under copyright independent of any photograph's own
license (Wikimedia Commons has actively deleted images of it for exactly this reason — see
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Deletion_requests/Aalto_vases). The interior example instead
pairs this real, verifiable product data with an existing tasteful lifestyle photo already in this
project (`public/assets/ikea/`), presented as an illustrative "seller's own photo" rather than an
official product shot — which is also a more honest representation of what a real secondhand seller
actually uploads (a casual home photo, not studio photography).
